Wayfinding Intersection Study

This online study looks at how people choose a direction at hospital corridor intersections. You will view a series of intersections on screen and select left or right, and answer a few brief questions about how you feel and how you find your way.

~30–45 minutesDesktop or laptop with a keyboardChrome recommended

Nothing that identifies you is collected. You are known in the data only by a study code. Questions: ma2236@cornell.edu
