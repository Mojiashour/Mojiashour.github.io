About me

I am a design researcher focusing on human-building interaction (HBI). I see people and buildings as being in continuous exchange, where cognition, affect, and the built environment shape one another as people move through, interpret, and make sense of space. I think of this as a dynamic interplay between our internal and external worlds. My aim is to advance a theoretically grounded understanding of HBI as it unfolds in situ, and to translate that knowledge into actionable insight for design practice.
