About me

I am a design researcher focusing on human-building interaction (HBI). I see people and buildings as being in continuous exchange: cognition, affect, and the built environment shape one another as people move through, interpret, and make sense of space.

My work asks how that evidence can inform design. It focuses on wayfinding and spatial cognition in complex healthcare environments, across physical, virtual, and augmented settings, using experimental, qualitative, and mixed methods. I have led studies within NSF-funded research and contributed to an NIH-funded trial, and I manage the Design and Augmented Intelligence Lab (DAIL) at Cornell. I have taught design studios, seminars, and lecture courses at Cornell University and Bilkent University.
